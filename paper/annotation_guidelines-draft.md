## Annotated Examples

The following examples illustrate how to annotate couplets with meter, rhyme, metaphor, and affect. Each annotation includes a brief explanation for clarity and consistency.

### Example 1

**Couplet:** "دل ہی تو ہے نہ سنگ و خشت، درد سے بھر نہ آئے کیوں"

* **Meter:** Ramal (common 14-syllable arud meter)
* **Rhyme:** AA (monorhyme)
* **Metaphor:** Heart compared to fragile matter (not stone or brick).
* **Affect:** Sorrowful, resigned.
* **Explanation:** The poet denies hardness of heart, likening it to vulnerable matter. This shows metaphorical framing of heart as fragile.

### Example 2

**Couplet:** "ہزاروں خواہشیں ایسی کہ ہر خواہش پہ دم نکلے"

* **Meter:** Hazaj meter (arud)
* **Rhyme:** AA
* **Metaphor:** Desires as life-taking forces.
* **Affect:** Longing, overwhelmed.
* **Explanation:** Each desire is imagined as potent enough to cause death, intensifying emotional despair.

### Example 3

**Couplet:** "چمن میں تلخ نوائی مری گوارا کر"

* **Meter:** Rajaz meter
* **Rhyme:** ABAB
* **Metaphor:** Harsh speech as bitter fruit in a garden.
* **Affect:** Defiant, expressive.
* **Explanation:** Garden (beauty, harmony) is contrasted with bitter speech, metaphorizing conflict.
